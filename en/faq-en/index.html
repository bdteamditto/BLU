FAQ

What is Blu Green Token?

Blu Green Token is a green token issued with the objective of raising funds to support the "Mangrove Reforestation Project for Thailand’s Sustainable Ecosystem”, under the Mangrove Reforestation for Carbon Credits Project (2022), covering a total area of 17,531.04 rai.

Why invest in Blu Green Token?
  1. It is a new investment opportunity that allows investors to participate in environmental restoration while receiving benefits from contributing to environmental sustainability, by integrating digital assets with the green economy.
  2. digital token that is approved  by the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC).
  3. Transparent and traceable through transactions conducted via Smart Contracts.
  4. Investors will receive their initial investment along with returns from net referenced carbon credit sales, which will be distributed starting from year 5 of the project onwards.

If the token issuer is unable to allocate returns from certified carbon credit sales as planned, investors will receive their initial investment together with accumulated compound return at a rate of 3.00% per annum, calculated on a daily basis from the project start date.

Where is the mangrove reforestation project located?

The Mangrove Reforestation project, under the Mangrove Reforestation for Carbon Credits Project (2022), covers a total area of 17,531.04 rai.

A total of 15 provinces:

  1. Krabi
  2. Chachoengsao
  3. Chumphon
  4. Trang
  5. Trat
  6. Nakhon Si Thammarat
  7. Prachuap Khiri Khan
  8. Pattani
  9. Phang Nga
  10. Phuket
  11. Ranong
  12. Rayong
  13. Satun
  14. Samut Songkhram
  15. Surat Thani
What mechanisms does the token issuer have in place to protect token holders?

     In the implementation of the underlying project, including the acquisition of the underlying carbon credits, the token issuer may be exposed to risk factors or unforeseen events, such as natural disasters, which may adversely affect the quantity of underlying carbon credits of the underlying project. In addition, the underlying project may be revoked, terminated, or otherwise disrupted, resulting in the token issuer being unable to continue the operation of the underlying project. The token issuer shall report any event that causes or may cause the cancellation of the token project, or the discontinuation of the project, business, or business plan related to the token, together with the reasons therefor, to the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) within three (3) days from the date on which the issuer knew or should have known of such event. Following an assessment of the impact on the underlying project, the token issuer shall compile the list of eligible token holders entitled to receive payment of accumulated compound return and initial investment. The issuer shall provide advance notice of the record date for the payment of accumulated compound return and initial investment to all token holders at least six (6) business days in advance via email and through an announcement on the token issuer’s website.

     In this regard, eligible token holders shall be entitled to receive initial investment together with accumulated compound return at a rate of 3.00% per annum. Such return shall be calculated on a daily basis from the project commencement date until the record date for the payment of accumulated compound return and initial investment.

When will returns begin?

The token issuer has a policy to gradually sell the referenced carbon credits, which will be distributed starting from year 5 of the project onward.

Therefore, during years 1-4, digital tokenholders will not receive any returns.

Who can invest, and is there an investment limit?

All types of investors are eligible to invest. Retail investors (who are not institutional investors, ultra-high-net-worth investors, or high-net-worth investors) are subject to an investment limit of not exceeding 300,000 THB per person.

How can Blu Green Token be traded in Thailand?

Blu Green Token can be purchased through an Initial Coin Offering (ICO) Portal. After the digital token is listed on a digital asset exchange, investors will be able to trade the token on the secondary market.

What are the risks associated with digital tokens?

Digital tokens involve certain risks, similar to other types of investments, such as market volatility and potential regulatory changes. Therefore, investors should carefully assess their risk tolerance and make informed decisions before investing in these digital tokens. Further information can be found in the project prospectus.
